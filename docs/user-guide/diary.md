# Diary

Open Diary from the top navigation and unlock it with your Diary password.

In the mood view, select a date and choose an emoji, write a short description, or do both. Save to record that day. Reopen the date to edit it, clear its mood, or delete the record.

Use the icon at the top right to switch to life events. The calendar keeps its month and selected date. Select a date to see its events or add one. Events can last several days, include times, repeat annually and appear in the countdown list. Mark an event as primary to prioritize its emoji on the calendar.

The countdown list shows the three nearest upcoming events by default. Select an item to open it. An annual February 29 event falls on February 28 in non-leap years. Countdown uses calendar days in your local time zone.
